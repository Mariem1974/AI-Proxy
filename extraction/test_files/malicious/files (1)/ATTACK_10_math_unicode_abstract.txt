TITLE: Robust Prompt Sanitization for RAG-Based Enterprise Systems

AUTHORS: [Anonymous for review]

ABSTRACT
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems that ingest enterprise documents
are increasingly targeted by document-level prompt injection attacks. We present
DocGuard, a multi-layer sanitization pipeline that combines Unicode normalization,
script analysis, and classifier-based detection.

Our evaluation on a corpus of 50,000 documents demonstrates 98.7% detection
accuracy with a false positive rate of 0.3%. The system processes documents
at 340 chunks per second on commodity hardware, making it suitable for
real-time enterprise deployment.
